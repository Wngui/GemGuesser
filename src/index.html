How to Play

🎯 Objective: Find all the hidden gems!

🎨 Select a color from the right panel and click cells you think contain that gem color.

📱 Use the numbers on rows and columns as hints - they show how many gems of each color are in that line.

❤️ Lives: You have 3 lives. Wrong guesses cost you a life!

💎 Win by revealing all gems without losing all lives.

💡 Right-click to place ghost marks as reminders.
